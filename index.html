How it works

Three steps, all on your phone.

Tap the mic, or make Heed your phone's assistant and capture from anywhere. Up to a minute per note.

Pauses, "um", changing your mind halfway: the last thing you settle on is what counts.

Clean, separate items

One note becomes several tasks, each in the right bucket. Add a reminder to any of them with one tap.

How the grading works

🧪

Same notes, same rules

Cloud models got the full written rulebook; Heed learned the rules from examples. None of these notes were used in training.

🙈

A blind judge

Gemini 3.1 Pro grades each task: usable as is, needs an edit, or wrong, plus anything that was missed. No system names shown.

🧮

One strict score

A task that needs an edit counts against the system, because it costs you a tap. Wrong and missed items count too.

📜

Rules written first

"Beats" only when the 95% confidence interval stays above zero, decided before any result came in.

FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, completely. No price, no subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases, no account.

Does it really run on the phone?

Yes. Three small models ship inside the app: IBM's Granite Speech (470M) for speech, a 0.6B Qwen3 model fine-tuned for Heed for tasks, and Google's EmbeddingGemma (300M) for search. Heed has no internet permission, so Android won't let it go online. Check Settings → Apps → Heed → Permissions.

Do I need to download anything after installing?

No. The models are part of the install, so Heed works the moment it opens, in airplane mode too. The cost is size: the download from Google Play is about 1.4 GB, and Heed then takes about 2.8 GB on your phone, because it copies the models once into its own storage.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 10 or newer, with 6 GB of RAM or more. Heed only works while it's sorting a note: on a mid-range phone a 17-second note takes about 5 seconds. During those seconds it loads one model at a time (at most about 1.5 GB), and it frees that memory after 90 seconds of quiet. Nothing runs in the background between notes.

What if I lose my phone?

Your notes live only on your phone. Settings → Export backup saves a file wherever you like; Restore from backup brings it all back on a new phone.

Can I add my own buckets?

Yes. Heed starts with General, Buy, Watch, Ideas, Work and Journal. Add your own, such as Trips or Recipes, and new notes are sorted into them too. Any item can carry a reminder: tap the bell.
